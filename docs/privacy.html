Proofr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version Proofr is built to be private. When you use the on-device engine, your text never leaves your computer. We don't run any servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't collect, store, sell, or share your personal data or your text. There is no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Who we are

Proofr is a Chrome extension that proofreads, rewrites, and translates text. It is developed by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). Contact: cjcarito15@gmail.com.

What data Proofr handles

Everything Proofr stores lives locally in your own browser (via Chrome storage). None of it is transmitted to us. We have no servers to send it to.

  • The text you select: used only to produce a rewrite. On the on-device engine it is processed locally and never sent anywhere. Text you select on a page is not saved.
  • Side panel history: text you paste into the Proofr side panel, and its result, is kept as a history list (the last 50 entries) in your browser so you can copy it again. It never leaves your device, and the trash icon in the panel deletes it.
  • Your settings: chosen engine, translation language, and whether hover actions are on, stored in your browser.
  • An optional cloud provider API key: Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint you choose to add. It is stored in your browser and sent only to that provider to authenticate your own requests. We never see it.
  • Usage counts: a local tally of how many rewrites you've done and which styles. It is stored on your device and never leaves your machine.

On-device processing (default)

By default, Proofr uses Chrome's Built-in AI (Gemini Nano), which runs entirely on your computer. Your selected text is processed locally and is never transmitted off your device.

Optional cloud processing (your chosen provider)

If you add an API key for a cloud provider (Groq, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a custom endpoint), the text you choose to rewrite is sent directly from your browser to that provider over HTTPS to be processed, and the result is returned to you. This happens only for requests you initiate, and only when a key is saved: always on the Cloud engine, and on the Auto engine whenever on-device AI isn't available or can't handle the request (for example, a language it doesn't support). Proofr is not an intermediary: the request goes straight from your browser to the provider you chose. That provider's handling of the data is governed by its own privacy policy (for example, Groq's or OpenAI's). We recommend not selecting text that contains sensitive information when using a cloud engine.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No collection of browsing history or page content beyond the specific text you choose to rewrite.

Permissions, and why they're needed

  • Context menus & scripting / host access: to add the right-click "Proofr" option, show results on the page you're using, and reach the cloud provider you choose.
  • Storage: to remember your settings and optional API key in your browser.
  • Clipboard: to copy a rewrite when you click "Copy".
  • Side panel: to show the Proofr panel when you click the toolbar icon.

Children

Proofr is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.
