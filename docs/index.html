Proofr

Proofr fixes the text you already wrote, in the box where you wrote it. Select it and press Alt+Shift+P (Option+Shift+P on a Mac), or right-click and choose Proofr. It shows what it changed, and Ctrl+Z puts it back. On computers that support it, Proofr uses Chrome's built-in AI, so your text stays on your device. No account, no usage limit.

Why not just paste it into a chatbot?

You can, and for writing something new, a chatbot is the better tool. Proofr is for the other job: fixing what you already wrote, many times a day.

  • No round trip. Pasting into a chat tab takes about ten clicks and keystrokes and two tab switches. Proofr takes two: select, then press its shortcut (Alt+Shift+P, or Option+Shift+P on a Mac; you can pick your own).
  • Your words stay yours. Proofread corrects grammar, spelling, and punctuation and leaves your wording alone. It shows exactly what it changed, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.
  • Nothing to sign into. No account, no message limit.
  • Private when your computer allows it. With Chrome's built-in AI, your text never leaves your computer. With a cloud key, it goes straight to the provider you chose.

What Proofr is not: a chatbot. It won't draft a cover letter or answer questions. It fixes and rephrases text you select.
