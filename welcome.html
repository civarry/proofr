Proofr is installed

Proofr fixes and rewrites the text you select, right where you're writing. Three quick steps and you're set.

1Pin Proofr to your toolbar

The Proofr icon opens its side panel, where you can paste text and change settings. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Proofr.

2Choose how Proofr thinks

Checking what this Chrome supports…

3Try it here

This text has a few mistakes. Press Proofread and see exactly what changes.

4Now use it where you write

On any page, select some text, right-click, and choose Proofr, then a tone. In a text box, your text is replaced and Ctrl+Z undoes it. With nothing selected, right-click inside a text box and choose Proofread this whole text box.

Or select text and press Alt+Shift+P to proofread it.

  • An email draft in Gmail or Outlook
  • A post or comment on LinkedIn
  • Any form or text box on a website
Proofr works only on pages where you use it, and stores your settings on this computer. Privacy policy.
